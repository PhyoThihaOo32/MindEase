What is MindEase?

Five features, one calming space

MindEase is a desktop Qt/C++ application built by YangonDevs for BMCC students. It combines campus resources, wellness tools, an AI assistant, and a private journal in a single thoughtfully designed application.

📚
BMCC Resources
Direct links to tutoring, financial aid, counseling, immigration support, health services, and more — all curated for BMCC students.
🧘
Wellness Toolkit
8 themed folders — from mindfulness and sleep to gratitude journaling and music — with practical tools you can use right now.
🤖
AI Assistant
Powered by OpenAI, the MindEase Assistant answers your wellness questions and guides you to the right campus resources.
📓
Private Journal
Write, save, and search personal journal entries. Export as PDF or plain text. Your reflections stay on your device.
⚙️
Settings
Light or Zen Night theme, adjustable font size, custom journal folder — preferences saved persistently across sessions.
🍃
Calm Design
Gentle falling-leaf animations, a warm natural color palette, and a quiet layout designed to reduce — not add to — your stress.
C++ / Qt Architecture

Built with OOP principles

MindEase demonstrates all four core object-oriented programming concepts required for CSC211H Honors.

🔒
Encapsulation
JournalEntry, JournalStorage — private data, clean public interfaces. The Screen base class hides m_title behind a const accessor.
🧬
Inheritance
Home, Journal, Settings, Recommendations, Toolkit, AssistantChat all inherit from the Screen abstract base class.
🔀
Polymorphism
MainWindow holds QVector<Screen*> and calls onActivated() + onThemeChanged() on each — runtime dispatch without knowing the concrete type.
🫙
Abstraction
Screen declares screenId() as a pure virtual — making it an ABC. Screen cannot be instantiated directly; every screen must supply its own identity.

MindEase Assistant

Your AI wellness guide, powered by OpenAI

Ask the MindEase Assistant anything about student mental health, BMCC resources, or wellness strategies. It responds with warmth and directs you to the right support.

🤖
MindEase Assistant
Powered by OpenAI · Here to help
🌿
Hi! I'm the MindEase Assistant. I'm here to help you find support, explore wellness resources, or just talk through what's on your mind. What can I help you with today?
🙋
I'm really stressed about finals and I don't know where to start.
🌿
I hear you — finals stress is real, and it makes sense to feel overwhelmed when everything piles up at once. Let's break it down together.

A few things that can help right now:
• Start with a simple list: what's due and when?
• Focus on the next 24 hours only — not the whole semester.
• BMCC's Learning Resource Center (Room S-510) offers free tutoring Mon–Thu 10am–6pm — no appointment needed.
• The Academic Coaching team can help you build a study plan: AcademicCoaching@bmcc.cuny.edu

Would you like help prioritizing your tasks, or would a study break strategy be more useful right now?
🙋
Is there free food on campus? I've been skipping meals.
🌿
Yes — and please don't skip meals. You deserve to eat, and BMCC makes it easy.

🍎 Panther Pantry — free food for any BMCC student, no questions asked.
📍 Room S-230
🕐 Mon–Fri 8am–6:30pm · Sat 10am–1pm

They have non-perishable items, refrigerated food, and frozen meals. Walk in whenever you need — no appointment, no paperwork.

The Advocacy & Resource Center (same location) can also connect you with SNAP food benefits if you qualify. Would you like help with that too?

Demo mode — in the real app, responses are generated live by OpenAI. For emergencies, call 988 or BMCC Counseling at (212) 220-8140.
